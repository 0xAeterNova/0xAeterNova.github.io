# V5.0 — NIGHT CIRCUIT

## Full visual rebuild
- Replaced the cream split boot screen with a coherent dark cyberpunk boot viewport.
- Added physical procedural city towers, transit lanes, rooftop beacons and orbital infrastructure.
- Increased and repositioned UFO traffic so it reads as part of the world composition.
- Added sentinels/humanoid cyber figures across multiple districts.
- Rebuilt lighting/material shader with fresnel, specular response and distance fog.
- Extended route-specific 3D districts with Forge, Arsenal and Missions.

## Interaction
- Rebuilt cursor as a laser-bracket targeting instrument.
- Added magnetic hover displacement and 3D card tilt microinteractions.
- Rebuilt Neon Atlas and Spectral Search presentation.
- Expanded keyboard route navigation to nine districts.
- Added Cinema mode.

## Transitions
- Longer cinematic camera arc.
- Rebuilt transition overlay with iris, horizon, chromatic blades, flare and scan geometry.
- Soundtrack is preserved; transition audio remains layered on top.

## Performance
- Retained adaptive DPR, particles and bloom.
- Added detail-level skipping for city/UFO/background geometry at lower FPS.
- Hidden-tab rendering pauses and WebGL context recovery remain active.
